# Job Posting

- Company: Ramp
- Role: Software Engineer, Production Engineering
- Source: LinkedIn paste provided in Codex chat on 2026-04-28

## Posting Text

### About Ramp

Ramp is building infrastructure for finance teams embedded in the transaction flow of business spend. The company emphasizes high agency, urgency, end-to-end ownership, and consequential engineering decisions tied directly to business outcomes.

### About Production Engineering

Production Engineering is described as Ramp's infrastructure ownership layer. The team is responsible for making Ramp faster, more reliable, and more scalable by embedding directly in product and platform problems rather than acting as an adjacent infra function.

The org's operating principles called out in the posting:

- One team, one company, one objective.
- If reliability or scalability is at risk, Production Engineering owns it.
- Move first and move fast when the path is unclear.
- Establish patterns and standards that become the foundation for the rest of engineering.
- Regularly recalibrate toward the highest-leverage work.

### Current Team Areas

- Compute: container orchestration, networking, load balancing, edge infrastructure, deployment systems
- Storage: databases, caching, object storage, foundational data infrastructure
- Workflows and Messaging: financial workflows and event-driven systems
- Internal Infrastructure: observability, cost attribution, CI/CD, and self-service engineering systems

### What You'll Do

- Build and operate critical infrastructure across compute, storage, messaging, and observability
- Own architectural change when reliability or scalability issues surface
- Partner with product teams during design to embed strong golden paths
- Contribute to Ramp's shift toward cellular architecture for scale, internationalization, regulated environments, and enterprise SLAs
- Proactively support AI-native engineering patterns and reusable infrastructure foundations
- Build developer tooling and self-service infrastructure for cost, performance, and reliability visibility
- Participate in on-call and eliminate root causes behind incidents
- Lead company-wide reliability and scalability initiatives across teams

### What Ramp Looks For

Mindset signals:

- Fix root causes, not just symptoms
- Think in systems rather than tasks
- Move urgently without creating chaos
- Treat product teams as users while still acting as an owner
- Leave every system and process better than you found it
- Be comfortable across database query plans, system design review, infrastructure tooling, and production incidents

Experience profile:

- 2+ years of software engineering experience on critical systems
- Strong software engineering fundamentals and production-ready code quality
- Hands-on distributed systems experience at production scale
- Experience with at least one major cloud provider, with AWS preferred
- Familiarity with observability practices such as SLOs, error budgets, alerting, and dashboards
- Track record of leading technical projects end to end with cross-team coordination
- Comfortable using AI tooling and coding agents in daily work

Bonus areas:

- Cellular or multi-tenant architecture
- Workflow orchestration systems such as Temporal
- Developer experience or internal platform tooling
- Fintech, payments, or regulated industries such as FedRAMP or SOC 2
